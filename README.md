## Overview

Two Tree Creative is a senior-led development studio focused on building, repairing, and shipping modern applications.

I work with startups, founders, and teams to take products from broken or incomplete → stable, usable, and ready to ship. The focus is always on practical outcomes, not over-engineered solutions.

---

## What I Do

- Build and launch modern web applications
- Repair and stabilize broken or underperforming apps
- Develop MVPs and prototypes quickly
- Integrate APIs and backend systems
- Improve performance and reliability in production environments
- Help teams move from idea → working product

---

## Approach

- Solve the problem first, not the architecture
- Ship fast, then iterate based on real usage
- Prefer simple, reliable solutions over complexity
- Use AI as a development accelerator, not a replacement for understanding
- Focus on systems that are maintainable and actually get used
